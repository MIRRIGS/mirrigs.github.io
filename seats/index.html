Seating Options

Find your seat.

Every rig needs a throne. Here's what fits a MIR RIG — and your budget.

Option 01

Used Car Seat

From ₹2,000

The smartest seat option for most people. Used car seats are built for actual driving, which means they’re usually more comfortable, more durable, and better for long sessions than most seats sold for sim racing. They’re also easy to find locally, surprisingly cheap, and can even be reupholstered to match your rig however you want. If you care more about comfort, practicality, and value than fake race-car cosplay, this is the one.

Budget-friendly Available locally Long-session ready
Option 02

Simulator Seat

₹7,500

The most affordable way to get into a brand-new seat with proper sim racing aesthetics. It bolts onto the rig easily, comes in multiple colours, and gives your setup that clean, dedicated racer look straight away. It might be the least comfortable option here, especially for longer sessions, but for people who want a fresh new seat without spending too much, it’s an easy entry point that still looks the part.

Option 03

Fiberglass Bucket Seat

From ₹16,500

The best-looking and most serious option, but definitely overkill for most people. Fiberglass bucket seats are rigid, aggressive, and give your rig a proper motorsport feel, especially if you’re running heavy pedals or just want your setup to look absolutely filthy in the best way possible. Prices start around ₹16,500 but can climb hard if you get into proper Recaro or Bride territory, so this is less about practicality and more about wanting the full race car experience.
